About

Capital. Operator mindset.

Brus Lai AS is a privately held Norwegian holding company. Our work is concentrated capital deployment across technology and the AI ecosystem, alongside a small set of owned software companies and games.

We're not a fund. We don't operate on exit timelines. Operating our own ventures keeps the investment thinking grounded in real product reality, not slide decks.
