Request data deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with you or your device from products operated by Brus Lai AS, including Vexsort: Color Puzzle. Your right to request deletion is guaranteed under GDPR and the Norwegian Personal Data Act.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to contact@bruslai.no with the subject line "Delete my data".
  2. In the message, mention which product the request relates to (e.g. "Vexsort").
  3. If you have your anonymous device identifier (a 32-character string stored in the app's local data), include it so we can locate the matching rows on our servers. If you do not have it — most users won't — let us know which device and we will guide you through retrieving it, or proceed with a general deletion request.

Email contact@bruslai.no

We will respond within one month of receiving your request, as required by GDPR Article 12(3). Most requests are completed within a few days.

What gets deleted

Deleted upon request

  • Any rows in our server database (Supabase) tied to your anonymous device identifier — specifically your submitted puzzle scores for Vexsort.
  • Any email correspondence you have had with us, after a reasonable retention period for record-keeping.

Not stored by us in the first place

  • No name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any personally identifiable information is collected by our apps. Nothing of that nature exists on our servers for us to delete.

Stored on your own device, under your control

  • Your local game history, streak, preferences, and the anonymous device identifier are stored in app storage on your phone. These can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device's system settings — we have no remote access to data on your device.

Automatic retention limits

Even without a deletion request, server-side gameplay data is automatically deleted after 12 months. After this period, no record of your submitted scores remains on our servers.
