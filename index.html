About

Long-term capital. Operator mindset.

Brus Lai AS is a privately held Norwegian company. We invest patient capital into early-stage software and digital products, and we operate our own ventures rather than treat them as portfolio entries.

Our work is unhurried, deliberate, and grounded in quality. We believe modest scope and careful craft outperform speed and noise.
